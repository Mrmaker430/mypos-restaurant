UrbanPiper acts as the bridge between delivery marketplaces and a POS. It supports Zomato and Swiggy restaurant channels, but a custom POS such as MyPOS must complete UrbanPiper's POS-partner onboarding and technical integration before orders can flow into this app.

What is required before live orders?

  1. Activate an UrbanPiper account for your restaurant and verify your outlet details.
  2. Ask UrbanPiper about onboarding MyPOS as a POS integration partner. Request developer documentation, sandbox credentials and a named integration contact.
  3. Complete Zomato and Swiggy outlet mapping in UrbanPiper. Their setup requires your active marketplace outlet IDs and URLs.
  4. After UrbanPiper approves the MyPOS integration, provide its official webhook/API specifications and credentials so the adapter can be completed and tested.

Live status: Not connected. MyPOS does not currently receive, accept, reject, or update marketplace orders. No test orders or fake API calls are generated.

Current MyPOS behaviour

Your existing billing, cash/UPI checkout, menu and thermal receipt features continue to work. Orders created at the counter are marked as POS orders. Zomato/Swiggy orders will only appear after the provider approves MyPOS and the real integration is implemented with their supplied documentation.
